Privacy policy

Privacy

Effective September 26, 2026

Keep ’Em Honest collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

What stays on your phone

The figures come bundled with the app, and the app sends nothing by itself, so nothing you do in it reaches us.

Your pins, cases and drafts are saved in the app’s own storage on your phone. Your phone’s backups include them, as they do for any app. Deleting the app deletes them.

Records requests

A records request in the app is a draft. The app never sends it. You copy or share it and send it yourself, to the agency you choose.

Links to sources

Opening a source link leaves the app for the publisher’s website in Safari, or in your default browser if you chose another. That website’s own privacy policy applies there.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and load their typefaces from Google Fonts. Those services receive what any website does, such as your IP address. The pages set no cookies and run no scripts.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
