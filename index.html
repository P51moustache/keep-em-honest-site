Help with Keep ’Em Honest

Keep ’Em Honest is an iPhone app for looking into city money yourself: the whole budget at once, what changed after the council’s vote, and who was paid.

Every figure comes with a receipt that leads to the public record it came from.

Questions, or a figure that looks wrong?

Write to us. If it is about a figure, say which screen it is on and what you expected to see.

In the app, Help has Request a city and Send feedback. Both open an email in your mail app that you send yourself.

Write to zlce.app@gmail.com

Common questions

Where do the figures come from?

From each city’s own published tables. For Los Angeles, those are the City Controller’s budget and spending tables and Checkbook LA, its record of payments.

Every figure in the app has a receipt: the table it came from, the day it was retrieved, and a fingerprint of the exact data. Tap a figure, then its receipt.

Does the app send anything?

No. The app sends nothing by itself. A records request is a draft: you read it, copy or share it, and send it yourself.

Does it track me?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your pins and cases stay on your phone.
